# Positioning — BundleGuard

## One-Sentence Specs (Decision Gate)

**Merchant pain:** Store owners lose sales and oversell inventory because Shopify Bundles shows wrong availability when components go out of stock, use "continue selling" settings, or sit in different warehouse locations.

**MVP feature:** A bundle health dashboard that calculates accurate per-location inventory, audits component OOS policies, and one-click resyncs bundle availability — so merchants always know why a bundle is unavailable and can fix it instantly.

**Pro add-ons:** Merchant AI for inventory Q&A, and Shopper AI (storefront widget) with name/email lead capture + CRM funnel.

### Description (listing body)

**Stop losing bundle sales to broken inventory.**

Shopify's default bundle tools leave merchants guessing: kits show "out of stock" when components are available, oversell when they're not, and ignore per-location stock.

**Core BundleGuard features:**

- **Bundle Health Dashboard** — See every kit as healthy, warning, or blocked — and why
- **Create & resync kits** — Build Shopify bundle products from components with editable quantities
- **OOS policy audit (Growth+)** — Flag "continue selling when out of stock" settings that break kit math
- **Multi-location audit (Pro)** — Find warehouses that can't assemble a complete kit
- **Blocked alerts** — In-app alerts; optional webhook when a kit newly becomes blocked

**AI (plan-gated):**

- **Merchant Assistant (Growth+)** — Ask natural-language questions about blocked kits and shortages
- **Shopper AI (Pro)** — Storefront chat with live catalog answers, image upload, and **name + email lead capture** with a CRM funnel (captured → pending → contacted → qualified)

Built for DTC brands selling gift sets, starter kits, and fixed bundles.

**Setup in under 5 minutes.** Connect your store, create or import kits, run a health check. Theme app embed optional for Shopper AI.

**Privacy:** `https://YOUR_APP_HOST/privacy`

### Competitive Positioning Statement

For DTC Shopify merchants who sell fixed product bundles, **BundleGuard** is the inventory reliability app that shows why kits break and fixes sync — with optional merchant/shopper AI. Unlike Shopify Bundles alone (weak location awareness) or discount-focused bundle apps (inventory is an afterthought), BundleGuard is built around accurate kit availability.
